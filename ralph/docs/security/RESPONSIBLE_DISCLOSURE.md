# Responsible Disclosure Policy

**Date:** 2026-01-22
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Active
**AC Coverage:** AC-SEC1.9

---

## Scope

This policy applies to all Robopoker systems, including:
- On-chain programs (entropy + poker)
- Entropy provider service
- Web UI and client SDKs
- Deployment/ops tooling

Do **not** test against mainnet with real user funds. Use devnet/testnet or local environments for validation.

---

## How to Report

Email: **security@robopoker.io** (placeholder)

Include:
- A clear description of the issue
- Steps to reproduce
- Impact assessment (what can go wrong)
- Proof-of-concept (if available)

If you prefer encrypted email, request our public key at the address above.

---

## Safe Harbor

We will not take legal action against researchers who:
- Follow this policy in good faith
- Avoid privacy violations, data destruction, or service disruption
- Report vulnerabilities promptly

---

## Response Timeline

- **Acknowledgement:** within 3 business days
- **Initial triage:** within 7 business days
- **Status updates:** at least every 14 days until resolution

---

## Severity & Rewards

Severity is determined by impact and exploitability. A formal bug bounty is not currently active, but we will acknowledge valid reports and may offer discretionary rewards.

---

## Coordinated Disclosure

Please allow a reasonable window for remediation before public disclosure. We will coordinate a disclosure date once a fix is ready.

---

## Out of Scope

- Physical attacks
- Social engineering (phishing, vishing, etc.)
- Denial-of-service attacks
- Known issues already documented in release notes

---

## Credits

We are grateful to the security community for responsibly reporting issues.
